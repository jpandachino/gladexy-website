Meet the owner

Glory Ogamba

Healthy living ambassador

Glory started Gladexy with a simple belief: you are what you eat. Every juice, milk and bake is made fresh, with real fruit and dates in place of refined sugar, so eating well feels like a treat.

From her kitchen in Lagos she makes juices, tigernut milk, smoothies and celebration cakes, and serves fresh juice at events across the city.
